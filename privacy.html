Privacy Policy

XBIHUB processes personal data under a privacy-minimization principle. Default node applications do not collect passport numbers, national ID numbers, health data, or other non-essential sensitive information.

Account email is used for verification, application status notifications, and agreement delivery. Data is retained according to operational and legal requirements. Cross-border transfers, if any, will be configured per applicable regional rules.

For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact the network operator through the official contact channels. This page is a baseline statement for V6.0 Signature Formal and will be updated under legal review before production go-live.
