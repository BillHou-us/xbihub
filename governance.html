Governance

Standards that Scale Trust

Clear protocols, auditable processes, and separation of duties — the foundation of a resilient global network.

Node Protocol

Every node operates under versioned agreements that define rights, obligations, data handling, and operational boundaries. Protocol updates are controlled and auditable.

Review & Activation

Applications undergo structured human review. Activation requires independent confirmation of signature, payment (where applicable), and readiness conditions — no single action grants operational status.

Audit & Accountability

Critical state changes are logged with actor, timestamp, and context. Role-based permissions ensure reviewers, agreement managers, and administrators operate within defined scopes.
