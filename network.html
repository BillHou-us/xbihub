Global Network

Distributed capacity under shared standards.

Nodes form the operational fabric of XBIHUB. Each pathway contributes sensing, processing, or resilience capacity while remaining accountable to network protocols, audit requirements, and lifecycle controls.

Pathways

Four entry pathways

Applicants select the pathway that matches their primary contribution. Review criteria and agreement packages are pathway-specific.

01

Commercial

Organizations deploying operational capacity under network standards, including facility, logistics, and service delivery aligned to XBIHUB protocols.

02

Research

Institutions and laboratories focused on extreme-environment biology, closed-loop life systems, and applied research that advances network capability.

03

Technology

Providers of edge intelligence, sensing systems, and life-support technologies that integrate with the network’s operational and data layers.

04

Community

Local initiatives advancing resilience, education, and public understanding in coordination with regional network priorities.

Lifecycle

From application to operational status

Every node follows a controlled path. Status is explicit, auditable, and reversible under governance rules.

01

Apply

Register an account, complete the pathway form, and submit for review.

02

Review

Structured evaluation by the operations team, with optional requests for additional information.

03

Sign

Execute the versioned agreement package through a verified electronic signature provider.

04

Activate

Upon satisfaction of signature, payment, and readiness conditions, the node enters active status.

Join the Network

Application, review, and activation are conducted entirely through the secure portal.
